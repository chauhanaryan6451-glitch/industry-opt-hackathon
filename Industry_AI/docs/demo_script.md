# 2-minute demo script

## 0:00–0:20 — Problem
“An SME food-processing factory knows its electricity bill, but the bill does not tell the plant manager which machine caused the increase or what action will reduce it without affecting production.”

## 0:20–0:40 — Solution
“FoodPulse AI adds a retrofit-first intelligence layer. It combines energy meters and process data with production and quality data. It learns expected energy consumption, detects abnormal behavior, diagnoses likely causes and recommends actions.”

## 0:40–1:10 — Live dashboard
“Here we can see production, SEC, energy savings, cost savings and equipment health. The compressor has a warning because its power is rising while pressure is falling. Refrigeration is also consuming more energy than its normal baseline.”

## 1:10–1:35 — Recommendation
“FoodPulse does not just raise an alarm. It explains the evidence and recommends an action. For the oven, the prototype identifies a more efficient operating point while enforcing a quality threshold.”

## 1:35–1:50 — Quantified result
“In our representative simulation, production stays at 5,000 kg/day and quality stays at 96%, while SEC falls from 2.50 to 2.18 kWh/kg — a 12.8% reduction.”

## 1:50–2:00 — Business
“The deployment is retrofit-first and designed for SMEs. We start with a small pilot, verify savings, standardize the sensor kit, and scale across food-processing clusters.”
